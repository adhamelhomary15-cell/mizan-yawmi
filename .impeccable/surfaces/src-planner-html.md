# Surface brief: ميزان يومي app (all tabs)

Scope: the whole app (Today, Week, Course, Tasks). Visitor mode: Operate. Platform: web, phone first, also laptop.
Audience and job: the owner, mostly outdoors in daylight, ticking blocks mid-day and reviewing at night.
Constraints: keep the name ميزان يومي; no Islamic ornament, no neon or glow, nothing that reads as a corporate app; no colour from the previous palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the day is a budget of hours, each kind of time a denomination with its own ink, the way every Egyptian pound note carries its colour. Refuses the grey planner with one accent.

OWN-WORLD: pale security-paper ground, intaglio inks (oxblood university, madder study, plum course, sepia work, ochre for gym, Quran and personal time), warm ink-black text, an oxblood shell (bottom bar on phones, side rail on laptops). Readex Pro for all text, Spectral bold for the large hour numerals. Fine single-pixel line work only, no ornament.

STORY: the visitor sees what is due now, taps it done, and watches the day's rosette fill; at night the rosette shows which half of the balance got spent.

FIRST VIEWPORT: oxblood shell; heading with the day and date, week arrows and review in the header; seven day chips; the now card with the block title, time left and a full-width done button; the target line below; the timeline starts under it. On laptops the rosette sits in the side column.

FORM: Egyptian banknote world, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed: none (unavailable). Signature move: ميزان اليوم drawn as a guilloche rosette, university and course on one half, work and life on the other, strokes inked per category as blocks get done, done hours as the centre denomination.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
